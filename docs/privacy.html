Privacy

This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you give us when you join the EzBiz early access list.

What we collect

When you fill in the early access form we collect what you type into it:

  • your email address
  • your name
  • your company
  • your trade
  • your team size
  • your state
  • what software you use now, if you tell us

We also record the date and time you signed up, the campaign tags in the link you came from (utm source, utm medium, utm campaign and utm content), and the address of the page that sent you to us, if your browser shares it. Those tags tell us which post or ad brought you here. This site uses no tracking scripts and no advertising cookies.

Why we collect it

We use it to contact you about early access to EzBiz, and to understand which kinds of contractors are interested. That is all.

We don't sell it

We don't sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

Where it is stored

Your signup is stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in the EzBiz Google account. Only the EzBiz team has access to it.

Deleting your information

To have your information deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email support@ezbizhq.com. We'll remove it and confirm by email.
